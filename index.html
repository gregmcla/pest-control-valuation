Speed kills (your competition): How faster lead response wins more jobs

What changed this week

Google now factors response time into local service ad rankings. HVAC businesses that respond to inquiries within 5 minutes are 10x more likely to book the job than those who wait an hour. New AI tools make instant response possible without hiring staff.

Do this now

1. Set up an auto-reply for every inbound lead that arrives after hours—acknowledge the request and confirm you'll follow up by 8 AM.
2. Use AI to draft personalized quote follow-ups for any estimate that's more than 48 hours old.
3. Create a simple "service reminder" text sequence for customers who haven't booked maintenance in 12+ months.

Steal this — instant lead response prompt

You are an assistant for [Your HVAC Company]. A potential customer just submitted a request through our website. Write a friendly, professional text message that: - Thanks them for reaching out - Confirms we received their request for [SERVICE TYPE] - Lets them know a technician will follow up within [TIMEFRAME] - Asks if the issue is urgent so we can prioritize Keep it under 3 sentences. Sound human, not corporate.

5-minute upgrade

Go to your Google Business profile right now and update your business description to mention "same-day service" and "fast response." Businesses with these keywords get 23% more clicks from local searches. Takes 5 minutes, costs nothing.
